### Renfeng Peng
Renfeng Peng is a Research Assistant at the [Department of Data Science](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/department/data-science), City University of Hong Kong ([CityUHK](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/)), working with Prof. [Fenglei Fan](https://fengleifan.github.io/Feng-Lei.Fan.github.io/). He completed his Ph.D. in 2026 at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science ([AMSS](http://www.amss.cas.cn/)), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), under the supervision of Prof. [Ya-xiang Yuan](http://lsec.cc.ac.cn/~yyx/index.html). He received his bachelor's degree in mathematics and applied mathematics from the [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://math.tongji.edu.cn/), Tongji University, in 2021. His research interests include tensor optimization, manifold optimization, and machine learning.
